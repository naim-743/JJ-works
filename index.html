OUR STORY

Engineering rigor, design sensibility.

JJ Works is a Dhaka-based design and construction consultancy offering integrated Interior, Exterior, Construction, and Consultancy services under one roof — taking projects from concept to completion.

Our work is led by a qualified civil and structural engineering team with expertise in RCC structural design, BOQ preparation, and code compliance under BNBC and RAJUK DAP 2022–2035 standards — so every design we deliver is structurally sound as well as visually compelling.
